## Design decision
The Balochistan city-only guide was removed as a primary navigation/page because it did not contain enough district-specific educational information to justify a standalone destination. Balochistan-first content remains where it is useful: scholarships, colleges, BBISE, admissions, medical admissions and official education sources.
